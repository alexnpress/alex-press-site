About Me

Alex Press is a journalist based in New York City. She covers the labor movement and working-class life in the United States. Her first book, What We Will: The Fight for Control Over Work in America, is forthcoming from Viking. She won the 2023 Bernhardt Labor Journalism Prize.

Her work has appeared in The New York Times, The Washington Post, Jacobin, The Nation, The New Republic, Bookforum, In These Times, and Jewish Currents. She also publishes The Alex Press.

She has hosted podcasts including Fragile Juggernaut, Casualties of History, and Primer. She has provided research assistance on feature documentaries, including Union. She is a co-organizer of Saturdays in the Park, a series of free concerts and community organizing fairs. She has also taught The Forge’s Writing for Organizers course.
